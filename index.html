About Company

Zinckon – Our journey started with a difference

Our name says it all

From the very beginning, Zinckon Universal has stood for the highest quality in zinc, iron, calcium, and magnesium products, proudly serving the food, pharmaceutical, and cosmetic industries.

Our products play an essential role in countless applications on the journey to the finished product—whether enriching food, fortifying supplements, or adding value in cosmetic formulations.

  • Providing personalized support to our global customer base
  • Certified, reliable quality, Fast, uncomplicated communication
  • Global sourcing with local commitment
  • Deep expertise connecting trusted producers to international customers

Our Commitment to Quality

We focus on delivering the highest product quality, dependable service, and exceptional reliability. Personal contact with our customers matters deeply, and our dedicated team is always available to support you quickly and professionally.

We are IFS Broker and GDP certified, and operate with a structured quality management system covering all responsibilities and processes.

We maintain strong, long-term relationships with our suppliers through continuous monitoring, visits, and audits. Complaints are handled promptly and used for continuous improvement.

Our actions are risk-based, hygiene-focused, and supported by a HACCP concept that undergoes regular verification.

We supply food, pharmaceutical, cosmetic, and technical grades of the highest purity.
